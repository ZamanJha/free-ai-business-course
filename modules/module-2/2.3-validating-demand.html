Validating Demand Before You Build Anything

Using your scraper's real output to sanity-check a niche before you commit to it.

You now have real data instead of a guess. Before locking in a niche in 2.4, run it through this checklist using the actual leads your Lead Finder pulled.

The checklist

  • Volume: did your search return at least 20–30 usable leads (real phone number or website) in your area? If it returned 4, this niche might be too thin locally.
  • Reachability: what fraction have a working phone number or website contact? If most listings are bare (name and address only), reaching decision-makers will be harder than it looks.
  • Underserved signal: click into 5–10 of their websites (where they have one). Do they have online booking, a modern site, obvious existing software? Or are they running on a static "call us" page — or no website at all? The latter is your signal.
  • Owner-operated: are these small enough that you'd be talking to the actual decision-maker, not a corporate office three levels removed?

Do this for your top 2 candidates from 2.1, not just your favorite — the data sometimes surprises you.

Copy this

Validation prompt paste into any AI chat, describe what you found 
I pulled [X] leads for [NICHE] in [AREA] using a business-listing search. Here's what I found:
- [X]% had a working phone number
- [X]% had a website; of those, [describe how modern/basic they looked]
- [Any other observations]

Based on this real data, help me honestly assess: is this niche worth pursuing, or should I test my second candidate instead? Don't be encouraging for the sake of it — tell me if the data is weak.
What you should have when this lesson is done A go/no-go decision for your top niche candidate, backed by real numbers from your own scraper output.

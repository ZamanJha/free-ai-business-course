Course / Module 2: Personalized Niche Selection / Lesson 2.4

Locking In Your Niche

Turning everything from 2.1–2.3 into one locked decision you commit to for the rest of the course.

Indecision here is the quiet killer of momentum. This lesson exists to force a decision, in writing, that you carry into every module from here forward.

Copy this

Decision-lock prompt paste into any AI chat, fill in your findings 
Here's what I know:
- Candidate niches and scores from 2.1: [PASTE]
- Lead Finder results and validation notes from 2.2/2.3: [PASTE]

Based only on this real data (not on excitement or gut feeling), tell me which niche I should lock in, and write a one-sentence definition of it including the specific area/region I'm targeting. Then give me three reasons I might be wrong, so I go in with eyes open.

Write the resulting one-sentence definition down somewhere visible. Modules 4, 5, and 6 all assume you have a specific answer here, not "local businesses in general."

What you should have when this lesson is done One written sentence defining your locked niche and target area — referenced directly in Modules 4 and 6.

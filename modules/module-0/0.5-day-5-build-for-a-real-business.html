Day 5: Build for a Real Business

Pick a real local business and build what they would need. No contact yet — just the build.

Before you start this lesson
  • 25–30 minutes
  • One real local business you can look up online

Today is the first time you build for someone other than yourself, and it's deliberately low-stakes: you aren't contacting them, you aren't pitching, you're just building what you think they need.

Pick a real one. A café, a plumber, a dog groomer, a driving instructor, a garage — anywhere you can see a website or a listing. Spend five minutes looking at how they currently take bookings or enquiries. Usually the answer is a phone number and nothing else, which is precisely the opportunity.

Today's build~30 min

A booking or enquiry system for that specific business. Their real name, their real services, their real opening hours where you can find them.

Specificity is what makes this exercise work. A generic booking app teaches you nothing you didn't learn on Day 1. Building one around a real business forces you to make the decisions you'll make for every client from Module 5 onward.

Day 5 build prompt paste into Base44, fill in their details 
Build a booking system for a business called [BUSINESS NAME], a [TYPE OF BUSINESS].

Their services are: [LIST THE REAL SERVICES AND ROUGH PRICES]
Their opening hours are: [HOURS]

Customers should be able to pick a service, pick a date and time, and leave their contact details. The owner needs a simple view of upcoming bookings for the week.

Use their business name throughout and pick colours that suit the kind of business they are.

Keep it, whatever happens next

You may never contact this business. Doesn't matter — this build becomes a portfolio piece, a template for the whole niche, and possibly the screenshot that opens your first real outreach in Module 5. Nothing you build this week is wasted.

What you should have when this lesson is done A published booking system built around one real local business, using their actual name and services.

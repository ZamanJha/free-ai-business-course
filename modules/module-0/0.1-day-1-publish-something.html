Day 1: Publish Something in 20 Minutes

No theory yet. You'll have a live URL before you close this tab.

Before you start this lesson
  • 20 minutes
  • A phone, to open the thing you build

No theory today. You're going to build something, put it on the internet, and be done in about twenty minutes.

There's a reason this comes first. You can't think straight about this business — what to charge, what to promise someone, how long a job takes — until you've felt how fast building is now. Read about it and you'll guess wrong. Do it once and it's obvious.

Today's build~20 min

A contact form for a business. Someone fills it in, you see what they wrote. That's the whole thing.

Sounds too basic to bother with. It isn't. A huge number of small businesses still take enquiries through a phone number and a notepad, and this is the thing they ask for most.

Paste the prompt below and let it build. Then change something yourself — the heading, a colour, whatever — so you've actually had your hands in the editor. Then publish it.

Day 1 build prompt paste this straight in 
Build a simple customer enquiry form app.

Fields: name, email, phone, type of enquiry (dropdown: quote, booking, general question), and a message box.

When someone submits the form, save it as a record I can see in a simple list view, with the newest first and the date it came in.

Keep the design clean and professional. Nothing fancy.

Publish it properly

Don't skip this bit. An app sitting in the editor isn't finished, and "I'll publish it later" is how most first builds quietly die.

Once it's live, open the link on your phone and fill the form in yourself. Watch your own message land in the list.

That's the part that gets people. Twenty minutes ago it didn't exist.

It won't come out exactly right

Something will be off. A field you didn't ask for, spacing that annoys you. Leave it today. Describing a change and getting it is the actual skill here, and you'll be doing it all week. Ship the rough one.

What you should have when this lesson is done A published contact form with a live URL. Open it on your phone and send yourself a message through it. Keep the link — you're coming back to this exact app on Day 4.

Day 1: Publish Something in 20 Minutes

No theory yet. You will have a live URL before you close this tab.

Before you start this lesson
  • An active Base44 Pro plan — this is the tool you will open every day this week
  • 20 minutes
  • A phone, to open the thing you build

Most courses open with a philosophy lecture. This one opens with you shipping something, for a specific reason: the tool does not make sense until you have used it, and everything in the next six modules assumes you already know what building feels like.

What you make today does not matter much. That you finish and publish it matters enormously.

Today's build~20 min

A working enquiry form for a business. Someone fills it in, and the submission is stored somewhere you can see it. That is genuinely all — it is also the single most-requested thing small businesses ask for, so it is not a toy.

Open Base44 and paste the prompt below. When it has built, change one thing — a label, a colour, the heading — so you have touched the editor yourself. Then publish it.

Day 1 build prompt paste into Base44, not ChatGPT 
Build a simple customer enquiry form app.

Fields: name, email, phone, type of enquiry (dropdown: quote, booking, general question), and a message box.

When someone submits the form, save it as a record I can see in a simple list view, with the newest first and the date it came in.

Keep the design clean and professional. Nothing fancy.

Then publish it

Publishing is the part people skip, and it is the part that matters. An app that exists only inside the editor is not real yet, and the habit you want is build → publish, not build → abandon.

When it is live, open the URL on your phone. Seeing something you made ten minutes ago running on a device you did not build it on is the moment this stops being theoretical. That reaction is the actual point of day one.

If it went wrong

It might not build exactly what you pictured. That is normal and it is not a problem you need to solve today — being able to describe a change and get it is the skill, and you will practise it all week. Ship the imperfect version.

Day 1 is the one that predicts everything People who build on day one tend to still be building in month three. People who spend day one reading tend not to come back. That is the whole reason this lesson comes before the mindset material rather than after it.
What you should have when this lesson is done A published enquiry form with a live URL you have opened on your phone. Save the URL — you will improve this exact app on Day 4.

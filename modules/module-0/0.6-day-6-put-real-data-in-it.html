Day 6: Put Real Data In It

Apps with real data in them are worth something. Empty apps are screenshots.

Before you start this lesson
  • 20–25 minutes
  • Your Day 5 build

There's a large difference between an app that could hold data and an app that holds data. Prospects feel it instantly, and so do you — a populated system looks like software, an empty one looks like a mockup.

Today's build~25 min

Fill yesterday's build with realistic data, and add a view that only makes sense once data exists.

Twenty or so bookings across the next few weeks, with realistic names, services, and times. Then add something that reads the data rather than just storing it: this week's schedule, busiest day, most-booked service.

Day 6 prompt paste into your Day 5 app 
Add 20 realistic sample bookings to this app, spread across the next three weeks, using the real services and plausible customer names and times.

Then add a dashboard showing:
- How many bookings are coming up this week
- Which service is booked most often
- Which day of the week is busiest

Make the dashboard the first thing the owner sees.

Why this is the lesson that sells

That busiest-day number is the kind of thing an owner has never had before. They know it roughly, in their head, and they have never seen it written down. When you demo in Module 5, this is usually the moment they stop evaluating and start picturing it in their business.

It costs you five minutes. It's the difference between "nice form" and "how much would this cost".

What you should have when this lesson is done Your Day 5 build, populated with around 20 realistic bookings and showing a working dashboard with at least three real insights.

Day 4: Rebuild Day 1, Better

Iteration is the actual skill. Today you practise it on something you already made.

Before you start this lesson
  • An active Base44 Pro plan — this is the tool you will open every day this week
  • 20 minutes
  • Your Day 1 enquiry form URL

Here is the thing nobody tells you about this kind of work: the first version is never the deliverable. What clients pay for is your ability to change it quickly when they say "could it also do X" — usually while they are sitting next to you.

That is a skill, it is learned by repetition, and today is the first rep.

Today's build~20 min

Three changes to your Day 1 enquiry form. Open it, and make it meaningfully better:

1. Add a status to each enquiry — new, contacted, closed — so it stops being a dumb inbox and starts being a workflow.
2. Add a dashboard view showing how many enquiries came in and how many are still unhandled.
3. Change something visual so it looks like it belongs to a real business.

Day 4 iteration prompt paste into your existing Day 1 app 
Update this enquiry form app:

1. Add a status field to each enquiry with options: New, Contacted, Closed. Default new submissions to "New".
2. Add a dashboard at the top showing total enquiries, how many are New, and how many came in this week.
3. Let me change an enquiry's status directly from the list without opening it.

Notice how that felt

Compare it to Day 1. The app got substantially more useful and it took less time, because you were changing something that existed rather than describing something that did not. That gap is where your margin comes from — and it is why the template work in Module 2 matters so much later.

Changing is faster than building This is the economic engine of the whole business. First builds are slow, edits are fast, and your second client in a niche is mostly edits. Every rep you do this week makes that gap wider.
What you should have when this lesson is done Your Day 1 app, republished with a status workflow and a dashboard. Same URL, visibly better tool.

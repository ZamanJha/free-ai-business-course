Day 7: Show One Person

One link, one real human, one honest reaction. This is the rep that everything in Module 5 depends on.

Before you start this lesson
  • 15 minutes
  • Your five published builds from this week
  • One person you can message

You have published five things in six days. Today you do the part that actually changes something: you show one of them to a real person.

Not a prospect. Not a pitch. A friend, a family member, someone you work with, anyone who isn't you. Send them the link and ask one question.

Today's build~15 min

Send one build to one person, and write down what they said.

Use the Day 5 or Day 6 build — the one for a real business — because the reaction is more interesting. Ask them: "I built this in about half an hour, does it look like something a real business would use?"

What you're actually testing

Three things, and none of them is whether they like it:

  • Does it read as real software to someone who wasn't there when you built it? That's the bar your prospects will apply in Module 5.
  • What's the first thing they ask? Whatever it's, real prospects will ask it too. Write it down.
  • How did it feel to send? If it was uncomfortable, that's worth knowing now, in a message to someone safe, rather than in your first outreach email.

Look at the week

Five published apps, one of them built around a real business with real data in it. Nobody who is going to quit this in month two has a week like that behind them — and you did it before the course has taught you anything about niches, pricing, or sales.

What you should have when this lesson is done One build sent to one real person, and their first reaction written down somewhere you'll reread before your first real outreach in Module 5.

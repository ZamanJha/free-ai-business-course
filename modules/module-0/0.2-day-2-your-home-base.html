Day 2: Your Home Base

A one-page site for the business you're starting. Rough is fine — you'll rebuild it properly in Module 4.

Before you start this lesson
  • 20–25 minutes
  • Yesterday's enquiry form URL

Today you build the page you'll eventually send to prospects. Not the polished version — that comes in Module 4 once you actually know how you're positioning yourself. Today is about having a home base that exists.

There's a second reason this is day two. Building your own site teaches you the same skills you'll use for client sites, on a project where nobody is watching and nothing is at stake.

Today's build~25 min

A one-page site describing what you do. Headline, a short section on who you help, a section on what you build, and a contact form — you can reuse yesterday's form logic here.

You don't have a business name yet and that's fine. Use your own name for now. Module 4 replaces all of this properly.

Day 2 build prompt paste into Base44 
Build a clean one-page website for a freelance software builder.

Sections:
- A headline and one-sentence description of what I do
- "Who I work with" — three short cards for different kinds of local business
- "What I build" — three examples: booking systems, client portals, internal trackers
- A contact form (name, email, message) that saves submissions I can see

Modern, professional, light theme. Make all the text easy for me to edit.

Fill in real words

Replace the placeholder copy with your own, even if you aren't sure yet. Writing "I build booking and enquiry systems for local service businesses" and disliking it's far more useful than leaving lorem ipsum in place, because it gives you something concrete to react against when you do the positioning work in Module 4.

What you should have when this lesson is done A published one-page site with a live URL and your own words in it, however rough.

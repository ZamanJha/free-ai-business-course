The Outreach Scripts

The exact cold email, DM, and follow-up sequence — literal text, not a description of what to say.

These are meant to be personalized, not copy-pasted verbatim to 20 businesses in a row — but the structure and the specific phrasing below is what makes them work. Fill in the brackets with real specifics about each business.

Cold email / DM opener

Outreach script — first message fill in brackets, personalize per business 
Hi [OWNER NAME] — I noticed [SPECIFIC OBSERVATION, e.g. "you don't have online booking on your site" or "your contact form just goes to an email inbox"].

I build simple software for [NICHE] businesses like yours — the kind of thing that usually costs $3-5k from an agency, but I can put together a working version in a few days so you can see it before committing to anything.

Would you be open to a quick 10-minute call this week? No pressure either way — happy to just show you what it'd look like.

Follow-up 1 (3–4 days later, no response)

Follow-up script 1 send if no reply after 3-4 days 
Hey [OWNER NAME] — following up on my note from last week. No worries if it's not a priority right now, just didn't want it to get buried.

If it's helpful, I can just build a rough working version of what I mean and send you the link — sometimes that's easier to react to than a description. Let me know either way.

Follow-up 2 (1 week after that)

Follow-up script 2 send about a week after follow-up 1 
Last note from me on this — I built a quick example of what I do for [NICHE] businesses, if you want to take a look: [LINK TO YOUR DEMO/TEMPLATE APP FROM MODULE 3]. No response needed, just wanted you to have it in case timing's better down the road.

Personalize with AI

Personalization prompt paste into any AI chat per business

Rewrite the [SPECIFIC OBSERVATION] part to be accurate and specific to this business, and keep everything else close to the original wording.

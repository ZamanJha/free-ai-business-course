What You Actually Are

The positioning that separates you from everyone else emailing local businesses about AI.

Before you start this lesson
  • An active Base44 Pro plan — this is the tool you will open every day this week
  • Your locked niche from 2.4
  • The builds you published in Module 0

Every business owner in your niche has already been contacted by someone selling AI. Chatbots, automations, "AI solutions for your business". They have learned to delete those, and if you sound like them you get deleted with them.

So the positioning question is not what you call yourself. It is what category the owner files you under in the first three seconds.

The category you want

Not an agency. Not a consultant. Not an AI expert. You want to land as the person who builds the software this business does not have — closer to a tradesperson than a marketer. Specific, concrete, obviously useful.

The formula that works is unglamorous:

The positioning sentence I build custom [THING] for [SPECIFIC KIND OF BUSINESS], usually in about a week.

"I build booking and enquiry systems for local trades, usually in about a week" outperforms "AI-powered business transformation consultant" with every single person you are trying to reach. The first is a thing they can picture buying. The second is a thing they have learned to ignore.

Where AI belongs in how you talk

You are genuinely in a powerful position: you can build in days what used to take an agency months, and that is real leverage worth owning. But lead with the outcome, not the method.

The owner does not care that it is AI-built. They care that it is fast, that it fits how they work, and that it costs less than the quote they got two years ago and never followed up on. AI is why you can do that — it is not the thing you are selling.

Use it as the explanation when they ask how you are so quick, which they will. "I build on an AI platform rather than writing everything from scratch, which is why this takes a week instead of three months" is a confident, true answer that makes the price make sense.

Positioning honestly with no clients yet

You have a real credibility problem and it has a real solution, which is not pretending.

  • Do not invent clients, logos, or testimonials. Beyond the ethics, it collapses the first time someone asks a follow-up question, and small-business owners in the same town talk to each other.
  • Do lead with what you built. You published five working applications in your first week. Most people pitching these businesses have never shipped anything.
  • Be straightforward about being early. "I am building my portfolio in [niche], so I am taking on my first few clients at a lower rate" is honest, it explains your pricing, and owners respond well to it. It is a genuinely strong position, not a weak one.
Specific beats impressive The narrow sentence feels like it is limiting you and it is doing the opposite — it makes you the obvious choice for a smaller group instead of a forgettable option for everyone. You can widen it in month six when you have proof.
What you should have when this lesson is done One written positioning sentence using the formula above, naming your actual niche. It goes in your outreach in 5.2 and on your site in 4.3, so make it one you can say out loud without wincing.

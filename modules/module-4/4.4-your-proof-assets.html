Your Proof Assets

Turning the things you have built into the three assets that do the selling for you.

Before you start this lesson
  • Your Module 0 builds
  • Your published site from 4.3
  • Your locked niche from 2.4

Proof is what you send when someone half-remembers your message and wants to know whether you're worth ten minutes. You need three things ready before the outreach in Module 5, and you already have the raw material.

1. The screenshot

One image of a build with real data in it — your Day 6 dashboard is ideal. Not an empty form. A populated system with numbers on it looks like software; an empty one looks like a template.

Crop to the interesting part. The busiest-day figure, the week's bookings. That's the bit that makes an owner look twice.

2. The forty-second video

Screen recording, your voice, no editing. Click through a build and narrate what the owner would do. Forty seconds, not four minutes — this gets watched on a phone between jobs.

It will feel awkward and the first one will be bad. Record it three times and keep the third. The reason it works is that almost nobody sends one, and it proves the thing is real in a way a screenshot can't.

3. The live link

A working app they can click and use. The single most persuasive asset you have, and the one your competitors can't produce.

Keep a demo build permanently live — a version of your niche template with sample data. Not a client's, not one with real customer details in it.

Describing work nobody paid for

Be plain about it. "I built this as a demo for [type of business]" is completely fine and nobody minds. What isn't fine is implying a client relationship that doesn't exist — and it's unnecessary, because the work itself is the proof. They're looking at whether you can build, and you can.

As real clients arrive, these assets get replaced with their work, with permission. Until then the demos carry it.

What you should have when this lesson is done Three assets ready to send: one cropped screenshot with real data, one 40-second screen recording, and one permanently live demo link. Everything Module 5 needs.

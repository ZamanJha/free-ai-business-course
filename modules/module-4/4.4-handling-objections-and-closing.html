Handling Objections & Closing

Price-anchoring against the agency alternative, and the responses to the objections you'll actually hear.

The same handful of objections come up over and over. Having a real answer ready — not improvised in the moment — is the difference between losing the call and closing it.

"That's more than I expected to pay"

Anchor against the real alternative: "A dev agency would usually quote $3,000–$5,000 upfront for something like this, plus weeks of back-and-forth. I can have this live in days for a fraction of that, and you've already seen it working, not a mockup."

"I need to think about it"

"Totally fair. What specifically would you want to think through — is it the price, the timing, or something about how it works? " — this turns a vague stall into a real objection you can actually address.

"We already have a system"

"Got it — is it actually solving [the specific problem they described earlier], or is it more that you're used to it? " — reference their own words from 4.3, don't argue against a system you haven't seen.

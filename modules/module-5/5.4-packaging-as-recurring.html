Packaging Agents as a Recurring Add-On

Turning a one-time build into monthly recurring revenue — the highest-leverage move in this whole course.

A one-time build fee is good. A monthly retainer on top of it is what makes this an actual business instead of a series of one-off projects. Agents are the easiest thing to sell as ongoing, because they need light maintenance and clients feel their value every week, not just once at delivery.

How to frame it

Don't pitch "maintenance" — nobody gets excited paying for upkeep. Pitch the ongoing outcome: "The follow-up agent is already recovering leads you'd have lost. For $[X]/month I keep it running, tuned, and add small improvements as your business changes."

Copy this

Recurring offer prompt paste into any AI chat 
I built a [DESCRIBE AGENT] for a [NICHE] client as part of a one-time project priced at $[AMOUNT]. I want to convert them to an ongoing monthly retainer for $[MONTHLY AMOUNT] that covers keeping the agent running, minor tweaks, and monitoring.

Write me a short, one-page pitch I can send after delivery that frames this as protecting the value they're already getting, not as an upsell they need to be talked into.

When to pitch it

Right after they've seen the agent work on a real lead — not at the original sale, and not weeks later after the excitement has faded. The moment it visibly does its job is the moment the value is obvious.

What you should have when this lesson is done A written one-page recurring-offer pitch, ready to send after your next successful delivery.

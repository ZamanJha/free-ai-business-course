What Happens After Client #1

Repeating the loop — and where the Module 5 recurring add-on comes back in.

Before you start this lesson
  • An active Base44 Pro plan — every build in this course runs on it
  • One delivered client build
  • Your niche template, updated with everything you learned doing it

Client #1 is proof the whole system works end to end. The mistake here is treating it as the finish line instead of the first lap.

Immediately after delivery

  • Send the 5.4 recurring-offer pitch if you haven't already — this is the highest-leverage single action available to you right now.
  • Ask for a short testimonial or a referral while the experience is fresh — both are far easier to get in the first 48 hours after a good delivery than weeks later.
  • Go back to your 4.1 prioritized list and queue your next batch of outreach — don't wait for client #1 to fully wrap up before starting client #2's pipeline.

Copy this

Next-batch prompt paste into any AI chat 
I just closed my first client using this process: niche selection, Base44 lead scraper, outreach, a sprint-week build and close. Help me plan my next batch of outreach — how many new leads should I contact this week to realistically land client #2 within a month, based on roughly how many replies and calls it took to land client #1 ([DESCRIBE YOUR ACTUAL NUMBERS])?

From here, the course loops: Module 4 outreach, Module 6 sprint, repeat — each time faster, because your template and your scripts are already dialed in.

Month two onward is where the year pays Client one was the expensive one — you were learning the tool, the niche, and the pitch simultaneously. Everything after it runs on the template, the scripts, and the pipeline you have already built. The plan you are paying for stops being a cost around here and starts being the thing the business runs on.
What you should have when this lesson is done A queued next batch of outreach messages sent, and (if not already sent) your recurring offer pitch delivered to client #1.

Course / Module 6: 1-Week Sprint Blueprint / Lesson 6.3

Days 4–7: Turning the Build Into a Sale

Closing with a finished product in hand, using the exact scripts from Module 4.

Everything here is Module 4, applied — the difference is you're now demoing their actual finished app, not a rebranded template.

Day-by-day

  1. Day 4: send the demo (reuse the 4.3 call structure) — walk them through their own real, working app.
  2. Day 5: handle objections using your 4.4 responses; if it's a yes, send the final invoice/payment request.
  3. Days 6–7: collect payment, run the 3.4 handoff checklist (custom domain if applicable, access, walkthrough), and formally deliver.

If it's a no

Not every sprint ends in a close. If this one doesn't, the built app isn't wasted — it becomes a second demo template you can show the next prospect, and you go back to your 4.1 list for the next lead. The build time was never wasted; it compounds.

Copy this

Close-day prep prompt paste into any AI chat before the closing call 
I'm about to close [CLIENT NAME] on a [ONE-TIME PRICE / MONTHLY PRICE] deal after showing them their finished app. Remind me of my top 3 objection responses from Module 4.4 adapted to this specific business, and give me one confident closing line to use once they've said yes verbally, to move straight to payment without losing momentum.
What you should have when this lesson is done Either a signed, paid client #1 — or a completed close attempt and a second demo asset ready for the next lead.

The Week at a Glance

How the build/sell split is structured, and why it's ordered this way.

Before you start this lesson
  • Your niche template from 2.4
  • A cleared week, or at least cleared evenings

This module assumes you've already got a lead who's said yes to a demo call, or ideally a signed scope doc from 5.5. It's the week where everything from Modules 1–5 gets used at once, on a real client.

The week

  • Days 1–3: build the client's actual deliverable in Base44, using your 3.2 template as the starting point.
  • Days 4–7: demo it (using 5.3), close it (using 5.4), and get paid (using 5.5) — selling a finished thing instead of an idea.

Building first and selling second is deliberate. A working demo is a far stronger close than a pitch, and because Base44 makes building fast, you can afford to build before the sale is fully locked in a way a traditional agency never could.
